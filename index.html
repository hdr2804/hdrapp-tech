About me

I'm HDR — an independent bug bounty researcher from Indonesia. My focus is smart contract and DeFi protocol security: staking, AMMs/DEXs, bridges, and on-chain reward systems.

My workflow is simple and never changes: the scope gets read before any recon happens, every finding is proven with a working PoC, and everything is checked for duplicates before it is submitted. A finding that doesn't meet that bar doesn't get filed — I'd rather finish a hunt with no report than file a sloppy one.

I also hold two principles that decide which targets I take on: no KYC and no submission deposits or fees. A few solid findings had to be parked or cancelled because of that — all of them are recorded honestly in the hunt list below.

How I hunt

Scope first, recon second

A program's scope page is always read first, before a single request is sent or a single line of code is touched. There is never any out-of-scope recon — targets outside the official list are not touched at all.

PoC is mandatory

Every finding must be proven with a proof-of-concept that actually runs — local Foundry or Vitest tests, not theory from reading code. If it isn't proven, it isn't reported.

Duplicate check before submitting

Before filing, every finding is cross-checked against public audit reports, changelogs, and issue trackers. One of my reports still ended up closed as a duplicate — that's an accepted risk, not a reason to skip the check.

No KYC, no deposits

I only hunt programs that can be submitted to — and paid out — without identity verification and without putting money up front. The consequence is real: several valid findings were never filed, and that consequence is written down plainly on this page.
